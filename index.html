FlashLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

FlashLock is a flashcard and focus app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information handled on your device

FlashLock stores your decks, cards, study schedules, preferences, blocked-app choices, blocked-website choices, and PRO entitlement on your device.

When you enable app or website blocking, FlashLock uses Android Usage Access and an Accessibility Service to identify the app or browser page currently visible. This information is compared with the apps and websites you chose to block. FlashLock's own code processes this information on your device and does not intentionally save or transmit the contents of your screen, browser pages, or Accessibility events.

FlashLock checks only for apps that provide a normal launcher activity. It does not request broad access to every installed package.

Information sent to service providers

FlashLock uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. When a crash occurs, Crashlytics may receive crash stack traces, relevant app state, device and operating-system information, app version, and an installation identifier. Google processes this diagnostic information as the provider of Firebase Crashlytics.

FlashLock uses Google Play Billing for the optional flashlock_pro one-time purchase. Google Play processes the purchase, payment, account, and entitlement information needed to complete and restore purchases. FlashLock stores a local PRO entitlement flag. FlashLock does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Sharing and exports

FlashLock does not sell personal information. Information is disclosed to the service providers described above only to provide crash reporting and Google Play purchases. Deck and card exports leave the app only when you deliberately use Android's share or export controls and choose a destination.

Retention and deletion

Decks, cards, schedules, preferences, and blocking choices remain on your device until you delete them, clear FlashLock's app data, or uninstall the app. Crash reports are retained according to the Firebase Crashlytics retention settings controlled by the developer and Google's applicable terms. Google Play retains purchase records according to Google's policies and legal obligations.

You can stop Accessibility or Usage Access processing at any time by disabling the relevant permission in Android Settings. You can remove locally stored FlashLock data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

FlashLock is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

FlashLock limits sensitive screen processing to the blocking features the user enables. Data sent to Google services is protected in transit by the security measures provided by those services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when FlashLock's features or service providers change. The date at the top will identify the latest version.
