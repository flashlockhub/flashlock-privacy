Request deletion of your FlashLock account

From the updated app

  1. Open Community account and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Select Request account deletion.
  3. Read the confirmation and choose Send request.
  4. If the app confirms Request received, keep the reference shown.

Your request goes to FlashLock's private support inbox for administrator review. The reference confirms that the request was recorded, not that the account or its data has been deleted. If the app says Request not confirmed, check your connection and retry. Retrying for the same account does not create another request.

Only request deletion for your own account. Signing out, removing the app or deleting local decks does not submit a server-account deletion request.

Without installing the app

Contact hello@flashlock.app.

Say that you want to delete your FlashLock community account. Provide the account's sign-in email or other account identifier and, if you already submitted a request, its reference. Never send your password, sign-in codes or access tokens. Any additional identity-verification steps must be proportionate and communicated through the confirmed private contact.

What the request covers

The request asks FlashLock to review removal of the account and associated server data: the authentication account, community profile, linked private submissions/files and account-associated service records.

Ordinary feedback is not necessarily linked to a community account. To request removal of a particular feedback message as well, provide its submission reference if available. FlashLock must verify the relevant record rather than assume that similar names, email text or message contents identify the requester.

The current request system records the account identity, reference, timestamps/status and available account/contact information for private review. Submitting a request does not automatically delete the account; fulfilment requires review and approval. The request system does not send automatic email acknowledgments. A verified email may be retained as a reply contact; providing an unverified email does not establish ownership of that mailbox.

Requests are reviewed by Flash Lock PTY LTD. We assess associated records, verify ownership and handle any published content or necessary retention exception before reporting the outcome. Email hello@flashlock.app with the request reference for status or questions. A receipt does not promise an automatic completion date.

Local cards and shared content

Your local cards stay on your phone unless you delete them separately. You can remove local decks or clear FlashLock's storage through Android. Existing Android backups and exported files may need separate action.

Deck copies already downloaded or exported by other people cannot be recalled. Tell us if your request includes hosted published decks, public attribution or earlier revisions. Their treatment is reviewed separately; a request does not automatically revoke a content licence or recall other people's copies.

Provider logs, Analytics and backups may require separate handling. Any retained information and its reason/period must be identified in the privacy policy; an upload-link expiry is not proof that an uploaded file was erased.

Status and contact

Keep your request reference and email hello@flashlock.app for questions or status. A request acknowledgment and a completion notice are different. Do not treat a receipt, a sign-out or an uninstall as confirmation of deletion.
